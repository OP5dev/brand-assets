## Visual direction: "High-Tech Zen"

Contrast sharply with the dark, neon-heavy "cyberpunk" aesthetic common in tech.

Colour palette:
- Base: soft off-whites or "Nord" inspired grays (very light slate).
- Accents: muted sage green, dusty teal, or a soft "blueprint" blue. Avoid bright reds or warning oranges.

Typography:
- Clean, wide-spaced sans-serif for headers (like Inter or Montserrat).
- High-quality monospace for code snippets (like JetBrains Mono), wrapped in soft-edged, low-contrast containers.

Imagery:
- Abstract geometry: slow-moving gradients or soft-edged architectural shapes.
- Photography: macro shots of organized hardware, smooth textures (brushed metal, stone), or minimalist workspaces.
- Diagrams: hand-drawn or "sketch" style architecture diagrams (Excalidraw style) rather than rigid, clinical icons.
